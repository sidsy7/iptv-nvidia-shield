## How to set up IPTV on NVIDIA Shield

The process is fairly routine.

First, open the Google Play Store on the Shield and install a compatible IPTV player. TiviLive's own Android TV guide points to IPTV Smarters Pro and uses the Xtream Codes API login method.

Next, open the player and add a new account or playlist. Enter the username, password and server URL supplied with your TiviLive account.

Give the app a little time to load the channel list and EPG. Once the guide appears, open a few channels rather than testing only one. Try live TV, VOD and a couple of different resolutions. The goal is to see how the complete setup behaves, not just whether one channel plays for 30 seconds.

For the network connection, TiviLive's installation guide recommends at least 15 Mbps and 25 Mbps or more for 4K/UHD streams, and says a wired Ethernet connection is preferred for stability. Its FAQ gives a similar recommendation of 25+ Mbps for 4K.

That part is worth paying attention to. IPTV buffering is not always a provider problem. Your Wi-Fi conditions, router, other traffic on the network and the particular stream can all affect playback.
